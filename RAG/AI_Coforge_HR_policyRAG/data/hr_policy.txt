COFORGE HR POLICY
(Fictional Training Document)
1. Casual Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for 12 casual leaves annually.
Leave approval must be obtained from the reporting manager.
 
2. Sick Leave Policy
Employees receive 10 sick leaves annually.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave
exceeding three consecutive days.
 
3. Work From Home Policy
Eligible employees may work from home two days per week,
subject to manager approval.
 
4. Travel Reimbursement
Employees traveling for official business may claim
eligible transportation and accommodation expenses.
Claims must be submitted within 15 days.
 
5. Attendance Policy
Employees are expected to complete eight working
hours per day, excluding designated breaks.
 
6. Training Policy
Employees are encouraged to complete mandatory
technical training assigned by the organization.
